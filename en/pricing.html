frequently asked questions

Before you ask for a quote

Why isn't the price on the page?

Because similar-looking systems can take very different amounts of effort. First we understand your case, then we give you a price that doesn't change mid-project.

Do I really need the diagnosis first?

No. If you know exactly what you want, we go straight to the quote. If it's still just an idea, the diagnosis usually saves more than it costs, because it keeps you from building the wrong thing.

Who owns the code?

You do. At the end of the project, we hand over the code, the data and the documentation. You are not tied to us.

Do you work on systems that already exist?

Yes. Many projects start by improving or connecting something that already exists. Rebuilding from scratch isn't always necessary.

How does payment work?

Diagnosis: fixed fee. Fixed-scope project: one installment at signing and the rest as deliverables are completed. Monthly partnership: a monthly fee for a block of hours.

Want the exact number for your case?

Tell us how your company works today. Within a few days you'll receive scope, timeline and price.
